A WAY OF WORKING

Notice first.
Make with intent.

Good work is a chain of small, explainable decisions. Here’s the one I’m practicing.

  1. 01

    Notice

    Look for the moment where a task becomes harder than it should be.

    ◎
  2. 02

    Frame

    Choose a real user goal and be honest about what is known and assumed.

    ⌁
  3. 03

    Shape

    Explore structure and interaction before polishing the surface.

    ▤
  4. 04

    Build & learn

    Make the design responsive, usable, and easier to improve with feedback.

A NOTE ABOUT ME

I’m Marcus.
Designer in progress. Builder by practice.

I’m developing a practice at the intersection of UI/UX design and front-end development. I care about clear flows, useful details, and interfaces that respect people’s time.

This portfolio is a learning record as much as a showcase. The concept projects are self-initiated, and any unvalidated assumptions are called out in the case studies.
